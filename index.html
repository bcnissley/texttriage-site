    How it works

    From missed call to handled — without picking up.

    No new phone system. No app for your customers to download.

    1. 1

      Missed call forwards to us

      When you can't answer, conditional call forwarding sends the call to TextTriage. Your number stays the same.

    2. 2

      We text back in 60 seconds and sort it

      The caller gets a friendly text in your voice. TextTriage figures out what they need and sorts it by urgency.

    3. 3

      Urgent now, the rest by morning

      Anything urgent reaches you immediately. Everything else arrives in a clean morning digest, ready to act on.
