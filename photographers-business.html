FAQ

Questions photography studios ask first

Do I have to change my number?+

No. TextTriage uses conditional call forwarding for the calls you miss, so your existing number stays exactly as it is.

What happens when someone asks about pricing?+

Only messages you've read and approved. We draft them with you during setup, and you can change the wording at any time.

What happens when someone asks about pricing?+

TextTriage sends your rate card link automatically and logs it in your inbox. You only step in when they're ready to talk dates.

What happens while the team's on a shoot?+

You set quiet hours and escalation rules. Booking and gig inquiries can still reach you immediately; everything else waits in the shared inbox.

We have a team — how does that work?+

One number, one shared inbox. Booking inquiries go to whoever's free; everything else waits for the team. $69 a month, about fifteen minutes to set up, no staff or new hardware needed.

When can I start?+

We're opening early access to photography studios now. Messaging goes live only after carrier registration and your approval of the message flow.
